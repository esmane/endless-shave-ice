how to play

Your goal is to guess Big Island Mike's favorite flavor of shave ice!

After creating a shave ice flavor combination, Big Island Mike will tell you how many of the flavors in the combination he likes. Get them all right and he will tell you his name for the flavor combination!

The correct flavor combination will not include the same flavor more than once.


controls

You select the flavor you want to place by using the buttons below the grid. Each flavor possiblity is represented by a color and a shape. Instead of having a different button for every single possible flavor, you select the color and shape compoment of the flavor separately. You place flavors by clicking on the grid. When you think you've got the correct combination, click the "Let's have it!" button to serve your creation to Big Island Mike. Mike will then tell you if he likes the flavor or not.


explanations for the settings

difficulty: This is how you tweak the difficulty of the generated puzzles. Beware: In master mode, Big Island Mike is not messing around. It will take an experienced shave ice connoisseur to guess correctly.

puzzle size: You can change the number of flavors in the correct combination.

dark background: When this is checked you get a dark background. If your browser is set to prefer dark pages this will default to being checked.

save settings: This saves all the above settings using cookies so your settings will stay when you close and reopen the page. If the autosave settings option is checked the settings will save automatically when the site is closed. If it isn't checked, then you must press the button to have your settings be saved.

about

It's a clone of the shave ice minigame from Nancy Drew: The Creature of Kapu Cave. I haven't seen this type of puzzle anywhere else so I made a browser version that generates its own puzzles. Have fun!
